# Suggested starter prompts

Use these in directory listings, the public MCP docs, the ChatGPT `defaultPrompt` list, and "try asking" examples. They match tools that exist today and do not promise email sending, prompt deletion, or an immediate monitoring run.

1. Give me a weekly AI visibility briefing for my brand: mentions, citations, trends, how I compare with competitors on monitored prompts, and what I should do next.
2. How has my brand's AI visibility changed over the last 30 days, and is the observation coverage strong enough to trust the change?
3. Which competitors rank ahead of my brand on monitored prompts, and where are they cited in AI answers when we are not? Which prompts should I target?
4. What are my highest-priority content and prompt opportunities this week? Draft tasks and new monitored prompts, but do not create them until I confirm.
5. Show my traffic from AI assistants in GA4 for the last 30 days, including landing pages, and separately summarize my Google Search Console organic performance.
6. Which monitored prompts had the biggest visibility gains or losses, and which engines drove those changes?
7. Which domains are cited most often in answers that mention my brand versus my top competitors?
8. Summarize our buyer themes and objections, then recommend content angles and tasks I can act on (show me the plan before creating anything).

The live ChatGPT plugin already uses three shorter defaults ("How has my brand's AI visibility changed over the last 30 days?", "Which competitors rank ahead of my brand on monitored prompts?", "What tasks are open in my Focala workspace?"). Keep those, and add 1, 4, and 5 when the listing allows more than three.
